Bull case
FedEx is generating strong returns on equity at 15.1 percent while maintaining a healthy 62.1 percent gross margin, indicating pricing power and operational efficiency in a capital-intensive business. The company is also growing revenue 7.7 percent year over year while keeping leverage reasonable at 0.81 debt to equity, creating capacity to invest in growth or return capital to shareholders.
 
Bear case
FedEx's recent 73.9 percent run means much of the good news may already be reflected in the stock price, leaving limited room for disappointment without a significant pullback. The company operates in logistics and shipping, which are cyclically sensitive to economic activity, and a recession or meaningful slowdown in freight volumes could pressure both margins and growth rates. Rising labor costs in unionized operations and potential softening in e-commerce demand represent near-term execution risks that could disappoint investors expecting continued momentum.
